D

How to join

View D · Takes one meeting

  1. Show up

    Come to any Tuesday meeting in Room 214. You don't need to sign up first or know anything about engineering.

  2. Pick a project

    Try a workshop, sit in with a project team, and join whichever one you like. Switching later is fine.

  3. Get shop-certified

    A 30-minute safety walkthrough unlocks the 3D printers, laser cutter and power tools.

E

Questions

View E · FAQ
Do I need any experience?

No. Most members started with zero experience. Workshops cover the basics, and older members pair up with new ones on projects.

Does it cost anything?

No dues. The club covers parts and materials for club projects. For personal projects, you only pay for materials you take home.

Can I come only some weeks?

Yes. Come when you can. Project teams do ask for a heads-up if you'll miss a build session close to a deadline.

I'm not planning to study engineering. Can I still join?

Yes. We have members heading into art, medicine, business and computer science. Building things is useful whatever you do next.
